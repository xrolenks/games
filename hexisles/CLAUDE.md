# Hex Isles (`hexisles/`)

**3D first, flat map simple (Rick's rule, 2026-11-19).** The 3D island is the main view; the flat (2D) map stays as a lighter fallback for players who want better performance, or when 3D can't load. Everything you need to *play* (spots to tap, pieces, the bandit, knights' strength and on/off duty, the longest route, previews, award banner, "+N" chips) must work in both. **Pure decoration goes in 3D only** (chimney smoke, flag flutter, dust, sparkles, weather, glows, floating Z's); on the flat map keep pieces still and plain. (Older 2D animations, like waves and grazing sheep, are still there; Rick hasn't asked for them to go.)

**Files (2026-11-19, Rick's request: split the code by job; was one `index.html`).** `index.html` = the page's HTML only (plus the Three.js import map); `css/style.css` = all styles; `js/` = the code, loaded by plain `<script>` tags at the end of `index.html` **in this order**: `rules.js` (rules data, geometry, ports, modes, cards, game state `G`, placement rules, `act`), `bots.js`, `draw2d.js` (flat SVG map + board effects: harvest, trade boat, knight chase, robbing card), `dice.js`, `ui.js` (saving, `render()`, windows, buttons, menus), `online.js` (PeerJS), `zoom.js`, `board3d.js` (Three.js), `sound.js` (plays the files in `sound-effects/`), `settings.js` ("Your turn" + Settings menu), `packs.js` (visual packs), `timer.js` (turn timer), `stats.js` (end-of-game stats), `tutorial.js` (How to play), `flair.js` (dust, harvest pop-ups, award banner, weather), `main.js` (start-up). `sound-effects/` = one .wav per sound. They share one global scope (classic scripts, `'use strict'` each). **Order rule:** code that runs while a file loads (e.g. `$('x').onclick = fn`) can only use functions from that file or earlier ones; for later ones wrap it (`() => fn()`), as `ui.js` does for `newClick` (in `online.js`). Calls made later during play can reach any file. Older notes below that say "this file" mean the whole game. Line numbers in older notes are from the single file.

Playable on one screen (pass the device around, or play against computer players) and over wifi with room codes (see **Wifi multiplayer**). Unlike Wild Wasteland, this code is written out normally (one statement per line, `//` comments are fine). Follows the site's originality rule (root `CLAUDE.md`, updated 2026-11-18): similar rules and features to other games are fine, and so are everyday words for cards and resources (knight, ore, paper…); the game's and modes' names, the art and the wording must be Hex Isles' own.

**Rules.** 2–4 players. Island of **19 hexes** in rows of `ROWS` **3-4-5-4-3** (Rick's choice, 2026-11-18; was 4-5-6-5-4 with 24): `TILES` 4 Jungle, 3 Mudflat, 4 Pasture, 4 Fields, 3 Cliffs, 1 middle land; `NUMS` 18 numbers. Geometry: 54 corners, 72 edges, 30 coast edges, 9 ports. Saves from the 24-hex island can't load (`load()` checks `g.hex.length`), so the New game window opens instead. The narrow 2D viewBox is `-285 -262 570 520`, the raider fleet sails from `FLEETFAR` (-310,-262) to `FLEETNEAR` (-205,-190); the 3D camera sizes itself from `ISLE`. shuffled each game: Jungle → Wood, Mudflat → Clay, Pasture → Wool (was Lagoon → Fish until 2026-11-17), Fields → Grain (was Grove → Fruit until 2026-11-11; `load()` renames `fruit` and `fish` in older saves: hexes, hands, ports, an open offer), Cliffs → Stone, plus one Volcano (nothing). Setup: in turn order and then back again, everyone places a town and a road; each starting town gives 1 of every land it touches. Then each turn: roll two dice, every hex with that number gives 1 resource per town and 2 per fort on its corners. A **7 is "low tide"** in Fire Mountain (no harvest, the roller picks one free resource) and moves the bandit in Classic (see below). Costs in `COST`: road Wood + Clay, town Wood + Clay + Wool + Grain, fort (upgrades a town) 3 Stone + 2 Grain. Towns can't be on neighbouring corners and (after setup) must touch your own road. **Trading with the bank:** `rate(p, r)` = how many of resource `r` player `p` gives for 1 of anything: `BANK` 4, or 3 with a town/fort on an "any" port, or 2 on that resource's port. **Ports:** `G.ports` = [{e: coast edge, r: resource or `'any'`}], made by `makePorts()` (one per resource + four "any", spread round `COAST`, the outside edges in order; shuffled each game; older saves get them in `load()`); `portAt(v)`; `usedPort(p, r, n)` = the port a trade at rate `n` goes through. Drawn by `port(o)` as a wooden **jetty** (Rick's request, 2026-11-17): a plank walkway on pilings from the beach out to a plank platform with the round sign standing on it; `portGeo(o)` gives its position and direction (`JETTY`, `DOCK` = platform middle, `BERTH`). Town 1 point, fort 2; first to `WIN` 10 wins. `MAXROADS` 15.

**Code.** Geometry is built once: `HEX` {x,y,v[6 corners]} (pointy-top, radius `S`), `VERT` {x,y,h hexes,n neighbour corners,e edges}, `EDGE` {a,b} (54 corners, 72 edges). The whole game is `G` (plain JSON, autosaved to `localStorage` key `hexisles-auto`, `ver` 1): `hex` [{t,n}], `pl` [{name,bot,res}], `b` buildings by corner {p,l 1 town/2 fort}, `r` roads by edge → player, `turn`, `phase` (`setup`/`roll`/`tide`/`discard`/`storm`/`main`/`over`), `su` (setup progress), `dice`, `win`, `log`. **Every move goes through `act(action)`** (`{t:'town',v}`, `{t:'road',e}`, `{t:'fort',v}`, `{t:'roll'}`, `{t:'pick',r}`, `{t:'trade',give,get}`, `{t:'end'}`, …), which checks the rules and returns true/false; `go(a)` = act + save + render. Keep it that way: wifi sends these same actions to the host. Placement rules: `okTown/okRoad/okFort`, `spots(p,what)`. Computer players: `botStep()` (on a `BOTPACE` = 1300 ms timer from `render`; was 700, slowed on Rick's request 2026-11-19), `botMain`, `vScore`, `bestRoad/reach`. Drawing: the board is one SVG rebuilt by `drawBoard()` (board pictures from `glyph(t)`: tree on Jungle, peak on Cliffs). Cards, trade buttons, cost chips and port signs use `cardGlyph(t)` via `ico(t)`: the same as `glyph` except wood is a pile of cut logs and stone a chunk of rock (Rick's request, 2026-11-17); glowing `.spot` shapes show where you can build and invisible `.hit` shapes (`data-v` corner, `data-e` edge, `data-h` hex) take the clicks. `MODE` = what the player is placing; `VIEW` = whose hand is shown. Windows: `#m-setup` (players, Human/Computer), `#m-pick` (low tide), `#m-trade`, `#m-disc`, `#m-win`.

**Fire Mountain mode** (only in **older saves** now: `G.fire`; no longer offered for new games). An original advanced mode: Rick asked for a "cities and knights" mode, which can't be copied from the existing board game, so this is Hex Isles' own take (a recurring danger plus something to defend with). The volcano is always inland. Every doubles roll adds to `G.rum`; at `RUMMAX` (3) `erupt()` picks one of six directions and lava runs in a straight line of hexes to the sea (`lavaLine(k)` using `HEXN`, stored in `G.lava` until the next eruption). Lava hexes (`scorched`) give nothing; a fort touching one drops back to a town unless it has a wall. **Walls** (`{t:'wall',v}`, `COST.wall` Clay + Stone, `G.b[v].w`, `okWall`) go on your own town or fort and are worth no points. Hexes beside the volcano are `fertile`: +1 resource per building. Computer players wall forts that lava could reach (`danger(v)`). Shown as a dark hex with an orange stream, a dashed ring for walls, "+1" on fertile hexes, and rumble dots under the volcano and in `#rumble`.

**Awards and player profiles (Rick's rules).** `awards()` runs after every successful move (the `act` wrapper around `act1`) once setup is over. **Longest route** (`G.lr`, both modes): the player whose longest unbroken chain of roads (`roadLen(p)`: no road counted twice, another player's town/fort cuts it) is at least `ROUTEMIN` 5. **Most Guards** (`G.la`, Classic only): most played (`P.kn`, `callers(p)`), at least `CALLMIN` 3. Each is worth `AWARD` 2 points (in `pubScore`). `holder()` decides: the holder keeps it until someone has strictly more (a tie changes nothing), and loses it if they drop below the minimum. The profiles (`#players`, one `.pl` per player: initial in their colour, points, resources/cards, route length and Guards played) sit at the **bottom right** on wide screens and right under the board on narrow ones; the player whose turn it is gets a faint tint of their colour (`.pl.cur`, `--c`); award badges `AWICON.route` / `AWICON.army` show on the holder's profile and move when the award does.

**Trading between players** (Rick's rules, 2026-11-16). On your turn (`main`): `{t:'offer', give, get}` (resource-count objects, checked by `cleanRes`; a resource can't be on both sides) sets `G.offer` = {from, give, get, yes: [accepted], no: [declined], x: [turned down by the offerer]} (`offerFix` adds missing lists for older saves; `offerState(o,p)`). Other players answer out of turn with `{t:'accept'}` / `{t:'decline'}` (`answer(p, t)`; `OUTOFTURN` lists the moves allowed off-turn, the wifi host uses the sender's seat). **Accepting doesn't swap anything:** the offerer then picks with `{t:'confirm',p}` (✓, does the swap) or `{t:'reject',p}` (✗) (`settle`). `offerDone()` closes the offer ("Nobody wanted that trade.") once no one has accepted and everyone has answered or been turned down. Computer players answer immediately when the offer is made (`botLikes`: they need spare cards, won't take fewer cards than they give, won't help someone within 2 points of winning) and never make offers themselves. `{t:'cancel'}` takes it back; nothing else is allowed while an offer is open (the bar's buttons are disabled then).
