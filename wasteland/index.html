WANDERER // A RARE OPPORTUNITY

You have wandered the wastes for as long as you can remember. You scavenge through old ruins and sell anything you can find to make a living.

One day while traveling, you explore a cave and find the entrance to a highly secured bunker. You heard stories about them, but you never thought they actually existed. While investigating, you hear a voice over the intercom. Stranger, our reactor system is slowly failing and we need a control chip to ensure this bunker's survival. If you can find the chip and bring it to us, we will let you join our community.

This is the opportunity you have been waiting for your whole life. No more selling scraps just for a meager existence. You unholster your old revolver and head out to the wastes one last time.
