How to play

Knock your rival's health bar to zero. Win two rounds to win the fight. Each round lasts 60 seconds; if time runs out, whoever has more health wins it.

Player 1

A D walk   W jump   S crouch

J punch   K kick   L block   I special

Sharing a laptop? F G H T work too. In 1 player, the arrow keys also move.

Player 2

Arrow keys to walk, jump and crouch

Number pad 1 punch, 2 kick, 3 block, 5 special

No number pad? Use , . / ' instead.

Moves

Crouch + punch: uppercut, knocks them down

Crouch + kick: leg sweep, knocks them down

Toward + punch up close: throw (can't be blocked)

Jump + punch or kick: attack from the air

Double-tap left or right: quick dash (punch or kick during it to dash in and hit)

Special: your fighter's energy blast, then a short wait to recharge

Staying alive

Hold block to guard. Blocked hits only scratch you.

Crouch to duck under punches and blasts.

Quick hits in a row make a combo.

Esc or P pauses.
