## Previewing / testing

The Browser pane's `file://` preview does **not** run the game's JS (buttons do nothing). Always serve over http. A minimal PowerShell server (run in the background, write it to the scratchpad), which also maps the `/games` prefix:

```powershell
param([string]$Root,[int]$Port=8765)
$l=New-Object System.Net.HttpListener;$l.Prefixes.Add("http://localhost:$Port/");$l.Start()
$t=@{'.html'='text/html; charset=utf-8';'.css'='text/css';'.js'='text/javascript';'.png'='image/png';'.svg'='image/svg+xml';'.json'='application/json'}
while($l.IsListening){$c=$l.GetContext();$p=[Uri]::UnescapeDataString($c.Request.Url.AbsolutePath) -replace '^/games',''
 $f=Join-Path $Root $p.TrimStart('/');if(Test-Path $f -PathType Container){$f=Join-Path $f 'index.html'}
 $code=200;if(-not(Test-Path $f -PathType Leaf)){$f=Join-Path $Root '404.html';$code=404}
 $b=[IO.File]::ReadAllBytes($f);$c.Response.StatusCode=$code;$x=[IO.Path]::GetExtension($f)
 $c.Response.ContentType=$(if($t[$x]){$t[$x]}else{'application/octet-stream'});$c.Response.OutputStream.Write($b,0,$b.Length);$c.Response.Close()}
```

Then open `http://localhost:8765/games/wasteland/`.

## Wild Wasteland: story and flow

You're a wanderer. In a cave you find sealed **Bunker 9**; a voice on the intercom offers to let you join if you bring back the **reactor control chip**. Title → Start → character creator (name, sex, hat, shirt) → S.P.E.C.I.A.L. page → intro → **Bunker 9** (cave; map node id `out`, used to be called "Bunker 9 Gate"). Step on a red **exit square** to open the **world map**; walk freely, discover locations, click one you're standing on to enter. The chip is in the **Old Power Plant** (node id `tower`). **Scraptown** (node `town`, next to the bunker) is a safe settlement with friendly townsfolk; the raider town further east is **Raider Camp** (node `scrap`). Using the intercom while holding the chip opens the door and wins.

## Code map (`wasteland/index.html`)

The code is compact: many functions are one long line. **Never put a `//` comment mid-line inside a one-line function** — it comments out the rest of the line and breaks the whole game. Use `/* */` or put the comment on its own line. Match the surrounding terse style.

**Grid & tiles.** Area is `W=26` × `H=20` (was 18×14: `restore()` rebuilds older saves' areas at the new size); index `ix(x,y)`. `T[i]` tile types: 0 floor, 1 crate, 2 cactus, 3 shack wall, 4–7 car halves, 8 rock, 9 bunker door, 10 intercom, 11 townsperson, 12 crop row. `items[i]`: `'s'` stim, `'a'` ammo crate, `'g:<gearId>'` gear, `'c'` control chip. `E` enemies, `P` player tile, `BD` bodies, `F` shack floor, `SK` shack.
- `newLevel()` generates desert areas from `lv` (danger tier); `buildCave()` builds the gate from the `CAVE` text grid; `buildTown()` builds Scraptown from `TOWN`. Townsfolk: `TOWNNPC` (sprite, role label) by layout letter; `buildTown()` makes `NPCS` (each with tile `i`, `home`, walk state) and `npcAt(i)` finds one. Farmers/villagers wander up to 2 tiles from home (`npcTick` on a timer, `NPCW` ms per step, drawn sliding in `drawIso`); guards and Alex stand still. Click one → `talkTo(i)` walks over, they say a random line from `TOWNTALK` (or their own `l` list, like the two Guards `G`, sprite `NG`, by the west gate) and hold still while it shows. The town is static: it's rebuilt from its layout on every visit and on load, never restored from a saved area. It has three sections in `TSEC` (`main` Market, `north` Homes, `south` Farms), each a W×H layout with its roofed houses in `hs` (6×5, 4×3 rooms; `houses()`/`houseAt()` also cover the desert's single `SK` shack); orange `O` tiles in the top/bottom wall (`LK`) lead to the section above/below (`goSec`, run from `render` via `PSEC` once the walk animation ends). `WMS.tsec` = current section, `WMS.tvis` = sections visited; on the world map, standing at Scraptown shows extra buttons (`#wmsecs`) to enter any visited section, otherwise you come in at the main gate (`enterArea(id,sec)`). **Alex** (`A`, sprite `NA`) is the trader, behind a wooden market stall lined up with the grid (`T` ×2 along x, tile type 13, counter facing +y; `stallBack()` draws the back wall and the inside of the left side wall at Alex's tile before Alex, `stall()` the rest of the side walls, the counter, posts standing on it and a short shed roof (`STR`, `roofZ`) with a STORE sign on top after; `B` (tile type 15) blocks the empty floor beside Alex); you're served from the spot in front (`sp`), and clicking the counter or Alex walks you there. Talking to Alex first opens a dialogue window (`openDialog(n,line,[[text,fn],…])`, `#dlg`: portrait, line, numbered replies, keys 1–n; reusable for any townsperson): "Looking to trade, stranger?" → trade, or "No, nothing right now" ("Come back anytime."). Trading opens `#trade`: your inventory | trade table | Alex's goods (stacked on phones). Drag or tap items onto the table (`OFFER`, `tMove`), then Confirm (`tConfirm`); the value difference is paid in caps. Alex has his own purse `WMS.ac` (100–200, set on first trade) and can't pay out more than that. **Rosa** (`R`, sprite `NR`, stands still in the Market) gives the necklace quest (`questTalk`, `nSay`): `WMS.q` 1 accepted / 2 done; the necklace (item `'n'`, icon `neck`) is put on the Raider Camp's east side by `placeNeck()` (in `enterArea`) until picked up; `WMS.neck` 1 carrying / 2 given back; reward `NECKC` caps + `XPNECK` XP. You can find it before taking the quest, and she takes it either way. Sprite letter `g` is never drawn in dialogue portraits (it's the guard's rifle), so don't use it for hair or faces. The town is ringed by thin adobe walls (`#`, tile type 14, `adobe()`/`wbox()`: a slab per tile joined to its wall neighbours), open at the west gate (pillars `GATE` + `gateArch()` with SCRAPTOWN) and the east exits. Currency is bottle caps `CAPS` (saved as `caps`, default 30); prices in `STIMP`, `AMMOP` (buy) and `CAPV` (sell value of gear). Sprites `NF/Nf/NV/Nv` in `SPR`.

## Saves

`snap()` / `restore()`; keys `bunker9-auto` (autosaved on every `render()`) and `bunker9-slot` (manual).
**Careful:** the autosave is loaded at startup (`if(auto&&restore(auto))…else newGame()`, near the end of the script). Anything `restore()` uses must be defined *above* that line. If `restore()` throws, it returns false and the game silently starts a **new game over the save** (this happened once when the S.P.E.C.I.A.L. data was defined below it). After restoring a test backup, check the last log line is "Session restored." and the HUD numbers are sane. **Old saves must keep loading**: when adding state, give it a default in `restore()` and migrate older shapes there (see the existing migrations for `ammo`→`AM`, road map→`seen/pos`, desert gate→cave, missing `time`).
